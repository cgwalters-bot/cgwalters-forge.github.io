## Releases

- **agentic-job**: a steady stream of `build-*` releases, 29 of them listed here, from [one on 25 October](https://github.com/cgwalters-forge/agentic-job/releases/tag/build-5e49c36781c0e671f394036c4591c4f0565be703211647670c9740325f624c38) to [one on 29 October](https://github.com/cgwalters-forge/agentic-job/releases/tag/build-597b943ea06a3e7920f57dcdaa8ffd442d33ae2c09365da414d8af7ee05f7fac). 37 further releases published in the week are not listed.

## Highlights

**bootc and bcvk.** bootc gained UKI addons ([bootc#2448](https://github.com/bootc-dev/bootc/pull/2448)) and `--from-delta` for `bootc switch` and `bootc update` ([bootc#2449](https://github.com/bootc-dev/bootc/pull/2449)). It now tells OCI manifest annotations apart from config labels ([bootc#2570](https://github.com/bootc-dev/bootc/pull/2570)), makes the storage bind mount namespace private ([bootc#2588](https://github.com/bootc-dev/bootc/pull/2588)), and rejects a config stateroot on composefs-only images ([bootc#2567](https://github.com/bootc-dev/bootc/pull/2567)). bcvk's ephemeral VMs can have a TPM 2.0 emulated by swtpm ([bcvk#411](https://github.com/bootc-dev/bcvk/pull/411)), and bcvk parses UKI files through a minimal composefs-boot dependency ([bcvk#397](https://github.com/bootc-dev/bcvk/pull/397)).

**A scheduler that picks work back up.** agent-board's pass now re-plans an open issue after its implement run ([agent-board#348](https://github.com/cgwalters-forge/agent-board/pull/348)), starts a P0 or P1 issue straight from a plan in its body ([agent-board#344](https://github.com/cgwalters-forge/agent-board/pull/344)), retries a planning request that came back empty ([agent-board#358](https://github.com/cgwalters-forge/agent-board/pull/358)), and revisits a sample of old issues when it has spare capacity ([agent-board#375](https://github.com/cgwalters-forge/agent-board/pull/375)). It also backs off on GitHub's secondary rate limits ([agent-board#359](https://github.com/cgwalters-forge/agent-board/pull/359)) and publishes its holds, WIP limit and pass summary for the review app to show ([agent-board#360](https://github.com/cgwalters-forge/agent-board/pull/360), [agent-board#373](https://github.com/cgwalters-forge/agent-board/pull/373)).

**agentic-job's apply moves to Rust.** The Rust apply work landed ([agentic-job#2148](https://github.com/cgwalters-forge/agentic-job/pull/2148), [#2161](https://github.com/cgwalters-forge/agentic-job/pull/2161), [#2132](https://github.com/cgwalters-forge/agentic-job/pull/2132)), and apply now routes neutral GitHub mutations natively ([agentic-job#2393](https://github.com/cgwalters-forge/agentic-job/pull/2393)). That is the base of the approved plan to split safe-outputs out on its own ([tracker#1047](https://github.com/cgwalters-forge/tracker/issues/1047)). The review now asks whether a change should exist before asking whether it works ([agentic-job#2193](https://github.com/cgwalters-forge/agentic-job/pull/2193)).

**Budgets and cache use at the broker.** The credential broker serves each consumer its budget grant and attributes usage per request ([praxis-credential-broker#42](https://github.com/cgwalters-bot/praxis-credential-broker/pull/42)), and agent-board takes its budget from that grant ([agent-board#345](https://github.com/cgwalters-forge/agent-board/pull/345)). The broker's proxy now forwards headers by denylist and logs prompt-cache use ([praxis-credential-broker#44](https://github.com/cgwalters-bot/praxis-credential-broker/pull/44)). Separately, the issue on the gap in prompt-cache hits between providers was closed ([agentic-job#2209](https://github.com/cgwalters-forge/agentic-job/issues/2209)).

## Lowlights

**A reverted wrapper move.** Moving the shared wrappers to agentic-job v0.6.4 ([actions#136](https://github.com/cgwalters-forge/actions/pull/136)) caused a morning outage on 29 October and was reverted ([actions#137](https://github.com/cgwalters-forge/actions/pull/137)). The re-proposal ([actions#140](https://github.com/cgwalters-forge/actions/issues/140)) waits for a canary that runs a real dispatch and review through changed wrappers ([actions#139](https://github.com/cgwalters-forge/actions/pull/139)), and the canary's trigger is still an open question for the operator.

**Refused fix rounds.** The 30–31 October reports described the poll repeatedly starting fix rounds on [tracker#1050](https://github.com/cgwalters-forge/tracker/pull/1050) that the policy step refused, because its branch was outside the fix bounds. At that point it needed a hand fix or a re-land, and held [tracker#1062](https://github.com/cgwalters-forge/tracker/issues/1062) by overlap.

**Waiting on people.** Several items stalled on the operator by the week's end. The first part of the Ubuntu 26.04 runner move landed ([actions#148](https://github.com/cgwalters-forge/actions/pull/148)), but the rest needs live Renovate proof before [actions#146](https://github.com/cgwalters-forge/actions/issues/146), [agent-board#555](https://github.com/cgwalters-forge/agent-board/pull/555) and [agentic-job#2400](https://github.com/cgwalters-forge/agentic-job/issues/2400) can finish. End-to-end runs against gitlab.com wait on a test account ([agentic-job#2110](https://github.com/cgwalters-forge/agentic-job/issues/2110)).
